# Mexican Visual Artists active during the Porfiriato (1876 - 1911)
The purpose of this project is to collect and relate data on prolific Mexican artists who were active during the dictatorship of Porfirio Diaz. The main focus will be muralist, painters and printmakers, due to how these techniques often found themselves between extremes - social protest, nationalism and the constant artistic influence of Europe and the United States - allowing us to observe social and cultural changes during a period of constant movement both within and outside Mexico.
